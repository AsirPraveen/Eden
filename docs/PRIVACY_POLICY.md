**Eden** ("the App") is a clinic inventory and prescription application operated by
**[YOUR NAME / COMPANY]** ("we"). This policy explains what data the App handles.

## Data we collect
- **Account data**: name, email address, and password (managed by Firebase Authentication).
- **Practice data** entered by you or your team: clinic details, medicines, stock, purchases,
  supplier contacts, payments.
- **Patient data** entered by the treating doctor or clinic staff: name and, optionally, age,
  sex, phone, address, clinical notes, and prescription history.

We do **not** collect advertising identifiers, location, or contacts, and the App contains no
third-party advertising or analytics SDKs.

## How data is used
Solely to provide the App's features: managing stock, recording purchases and payments,
creating and printing prescriptions, and showing reports to the practice that owns the data.

## Storage and security
Data is stored in Google Firebase (Cloud Firestore), encrypted in transit and at rest.
Access is restricted by security rules so that only authenticated members of a practice can
read or write that practice's data. Every stock and payment change is recorded in an
audit log visible to the practice.

## Patient data responsibility
The doctor/practice using Eden is the data fiduciary for patient records under India's
Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023. We process this data only on the practice's
behalf. Doctors should obtain patient consent before recording their details.

## Data sharing
We do not sell, rent, or share any data with third parties. Data resides with Google Cloud
(Firebase) as our infrastructure provider, subject to Google's security and privacy terms.

## Retention and deletion
Data is retained while the practice's account is active. A practice may request export or
permanent deletion of all its data by contacting **[SUPPORT EMAIL]**; deletion is completed
within 30 days. Individual patient records can be deleted in-app by practice managers.

## Children
Eden is a professional tool for medical practices and is not directed at children. Patient
records of minors are managed by the treating doctor under their duty of care.

## Changes
We may update this policy; material changes will be notified in the App.
