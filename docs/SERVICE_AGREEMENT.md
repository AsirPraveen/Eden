## 1. Scope of work
Developer provides the "Eden" mobile application: clinic inventory management, purchase and
rep-payment tracking, patient records, prescription creation with thermal printing, reports,
and multi-user access - as described in the attached feature list (Annex A).

## 2. Fees and payment
- Development fee: ₹[AMOUNT], payable [e.g. 30% on signing, 40% on beta, 30% on delivery].
- Infrastructure: the app runs on the Client's own Firebase project (free tier). Any future
  Firebase charges (if usage exceeds the free tier) are borne by the Client.
- Play Store publication (optional): Client creates and owns the developer account ($25).

## 3. Delivery and acceptance
Delivery is an installable APK plus the configured Firebase project. Client has [14] days to
report defects; Developer will fix reproducible defects free of charge for [30] days after
acceptance. Further changes are charged at ₹[RATE].

## 4. Intellectual property
[Choose one]
- **Option A (client-owned):** On full payment, the source code for this delivery is assigned
  to the Client.
- **Option B (license, recommended for SaaS):** Developer retains ownership of the Eden
  codebase; Client receives a perpetual, non-exclusive license to use the app. Developer may
  offer Eden to other practices.

## 5. Data protection (DPDP Act 2023)
- Client is the **data fiduciary** for all patient data; Developer is a **data processor**.
- Developer will access practice data only for support, at the Client's request, and will keep
  it confidential.
- Data is stored in the Client's Firebase project; on termination, the Client keeps the
  project and all data. Developer's access is removed at handover.
- Both parties will report any data breach to the other without undue delay.

## 6. Warranties and liability
The app is provided without warranty of fitness for a particular purpose. Clinical use,
prescription content, and statutory compliance remain the Client's responsibility. Developer's
total liability is capped at the fees paid under this agreement.

## 7. Termination
Either party may terminate with [30] days' written notice. Clauses 4–6 survive termination.
